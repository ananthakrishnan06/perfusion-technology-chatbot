Clinical Science & Curriculum

What is Perfusion Technology?

Core Curriculum Domains

Perfusion Technology is the specialized clinical discipline dedicated to operating extracorporeal circulation (ECC) systems—principally the Heart-Lung Machine (HLM). During open-heart and thoracic surgical procedures, the patient's heart must be temporarily arrested and the lungs immobilized. The clinical perfusionist assumes continuous responsibility for the patient's artificial respiration, systemic blood circulation, oxygen delivery, and organ preservation.

01. Gas Exchange

Membrane Oxygenation

Continuous transfer of O₂ and elimination of CO₂ across microporous hollow-fiber membranes.

02. Anticoagulation

ACT Monitoring (>480s)

Systemic heparinization preventing circuit thrombosis and precise protamine neutralization.

03. Protection

Cardioplegic Arrest

Infusing hyperkalemic hypothermic or warm blood cardioplegia to preserve ischemic myocardium.

04. Hemodynamics

DO₂ & SVR Balance

Managing pump flow rates, line pressures, systemic vascular resistance, and target organ perfusion.
